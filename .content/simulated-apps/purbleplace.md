### The mini-games

Purble Place is a suite of three mini-games. Each one has its own rules, difficulty levels, and statistics:

- [Purble Pairs](./purblepairs) - flip the tiles and find every matching pair before the time runs out.
- [Comfy Cakes](./comfycakes) - build each cake to match the order shown on the conveyor belt.
- [Purble Shop](./purbleshop) - work out the hidden combination of features and dress up the Purble.
