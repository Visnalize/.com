# Introducing Win7 Simu Supporter

![Win7 Simu Supporter](/assets/covers/win7-simu-supporter.jpg)

_Cover image designed on Pawel Czerwinski's original photo from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/photos/turned-off-signage-_9dSF0Hwitw)_

If you came here from the notification inside the app, hello there 👋 and thanks for clicking (I know most people just close these off, so it means a lot). Starting from version [4.8.1](/win7simu/changelog.md), we've added a new pricing tier for [Win7 Simu](/win7simu/about.md) called __Supporter__. In this post, I want to tell you what it is, what you get from it, and more importantly, why it exists in the first place. So bear with me for a few minutes.

## What is Supporter?

In short, Supporter is a __one-time purchase__ that gives you everything the Lifetime plan gives, plus access to a __private Discord__ where you can talk to me directly.

* No ads, forever.
* All paid themes unlocked, including the ones added in the future.
* A private Discord channel for Supporters only.
* Priority support and priority on your feature requests.

It costs __$99__, paid once, no subscription. On the web, the price is shown in your local currency and adjusted for your country, so what you see in the app may be a bit different.

:::tip Already a Lifetime owner?
Nothing changes for you, Lifetime still removes ads and unlocks all paid themes as before. Supporter is just Lifetime with the Discord on top, and a bigger "thank you" to me.
:::

## Why the private Discord?

Over the years, I have received a lot of emails, reviews and comments from users, some with bug reports, some with feature ideas, some just to say hi. I read all of them, I really do, but to be honest, as one guy working on this project over the weekend, I cannot reply to everyone, and many good ideas get buried under a pile of other things. It's not fair to the people who took their time to write to me, and I feel bad about it.

The private Discord is my attempt to fix that, at least for a small group of people. It's a place where I can actually have a conversation with you, not a one-way message that goes into the void. If something is broken for you, you get help first. If you have an idea for a feature, I will look at it first when I plan the next updates on my [feature board](https://github.com/orgs/Visnalize/projects/1). And if you just want to chat about Windows 7 and the good old days, well, that's welcome too.

## Why now?

If you have read [the second part](./building-win7-simu-pt-2.md) of my journey with Win7 Simu, you know that money is a big reason why I keep this project going. I'm not going to pretend otherwise. The server for the [web browser](./building-win7-simu.md#iframe-issue), the domain, the tools, the many many hours I spend on each update, all of them cost something. Ads cover a part of it, but I have always wanted to rely on them [less](./about-the-ads-2.md), not more.

Supporter is my way to move a bit further in that direction. Instead of showing you more ads, I would rather offer something that is actually useful to you, a direct line to me, in exchange for helping the project keep going.

___So, is it worth $99?___

Let's be real, it's not a small amount of money, and I totally understand if it's not for you. Win7 Simu stays free, all the core features stay free, and I will keep releasing updates for everyone, whether you buy anything or not. That's a promise. But if Win7 Simu has been with you for a while, brought back some memories, helped you learn or teach about computers, or simply made a boring day a little less boring, and you are in a position to give back, Supporter is the best way to do it. Every purchase goes directly into keeping this project alive and making it better.

## How to become a Supporter

1. Open Win7 Simu, on [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.visnalize.win7simu) or on the [web](https://win7simu.visnalize.com).
2. Click __Remove ads__ on any ad (or the watermark on the desktop) to open the pricing panel.
3. Pick __Supporter__ and complete the payment.
4. After that, a welcome screen will show up with 3 simple steps: copy your member ID, join the Discord, then open a ticket in `#verify` and send your member ID to get access.

If you close the welcome screen before finishing, don't worry, you can open it again any time from the __About__ window in the app.
