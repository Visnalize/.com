## 2026

### `1.1.0` (Nov 9, 2026)

- Play now opens a game picker on the pal's screen. Press Next to see the next game and Select to start it. The Back card returns to the pal.
- 9 minigames, all played with the two shell buttons: Left Right, Hi Lo, Copy Me and Sticks are free; Racing, Catch, Snake, Jump and Stack need Pro.
- Every finished game counts as Play, won or lost. A win makes the pal jump.
- The game-over screen shows the score and the best score, with Again and Quit.
- A new Games section on the Pal tab lets you choose 1 to 4 games for Play. Locked Pro games open Pro.
- The Pal widget can play the free games too, one tap per move.
- A daily challenge: one chosen game a day with a goal. The care card shows it and how many days in a row you did it.
- Your pal calls for care when it is hungry, bored or has made a mess. A call left for 30 minutes counts as a miss.
- Misses while growing up decide the adult form: Secret (no misses), Bright, or Scruffy.
- At night the pal falls asleep. The Play button becomes a Lights switch, and with the lights off the screen dims in the app and widgets. Lights left on for 30 minutes are a miss.
- With the lights off, the Live Activity dims too. <Notation icon="ios" />
- With the lights off, the live wallpaper dims too. <Notation icon="android" />
- A sleeping pal cannot play. Only Feed wakes it.
- A week after growing up, the adult leaves on a journey and a paw waves goodbye. You can hatch the next generation, and every pal that left is kept in an album.
- New Info sheet with age, generation, misses and form. It also shows your pal's stages, how many days of care are left until it is an adult, and the forms you can aim for: Bright and a secret one.
- Beeps for care, games (each point and miss), hatching, growing up and the journey, plus a beeping reminder. Turn them off in Settings > Sounds; reminders then use the phone's usual sound.
- The share card shows your best game and score, an adult's form, a star for the Secret form, and the generation.
- While the egg hatches, a card tells you that tapping Feed or Play makes it hatch sooner.
- When your pal hatches, the celebration offers "Play a game" right away.
- The home screen widget tip now appears after the hatch, not before.
- Every growing-up celebration tells you how many days of care are left until the next stage.
- Brick Pals now runs on iPad, in portrait and landscape. <Notation icon="ios" />
- New extra-large Pal widget on iPad. <Notation icon="ios" />
- Wallpapers on iPad fit both orientations, and the thumbnails show what stays visible. <Notation icon="ios" />
- On iPad, "Take Along" mentions only the Lock Screen, because iPad has no Dynamic Island. <Notation icon="ios" />
- Phones can turn to landscape. The pal sits on the left and the care card on the right.
- On tablets and in landscape, the pal stays in place while you scroll the care card.
- Android tablets and phones in landscape show the tabs on the side. <Notation icon="android" />
- Screens stay at a comfortable width on tablets instead of stretching.
- The Android live wallpaper keeps its pixel size when the screen turns. <Notation icon="android" />
- Play on an unhatched egg on the widget does nothing.
- Players updating from the previous version start with no misses.
