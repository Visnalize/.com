# Marketing

As an indie maker, I enjoy building a product more than anything.
I realized a fact, if I want to get sales and grow my product, people need to know it exists.

But I don't like marketing. At all.
Not because I'm shy or afraid to talk about my projects.
It's because, the more I'm on this side, the more I realize, marketing involves gimmicks or sometimes blatant lies to capture people's attention.
