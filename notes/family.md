# Family

We went to the church yesterday, my parents, wife, son, and I.

My son was being mischievous as usual (in a good way), and we couldn't focus.
I scolded him a bit, but my parents didn't mind, held him and laughed at him, most of the time.

For a moment, I nearly shed tears, the tears of joy. I felt truly blessed, for my son and myself.
