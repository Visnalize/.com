# Titles

I never paid any attention to this before, I guess because everyone does it, it becomes normal.
But somehow, lately, I've begun to realize, how obsessive people (including _myself_) are over **titles**.

"Software Engineer, Product Designer, Problem Solver, Tech Influencer, Entrepreneur..." ⁉

Well, I guess those sound way bigger and fancier. But, have you told them to people outside of your bubble (industry)?
What's the point if they just don't get it, and you have to start explaining those fancy titles in a common sense?
