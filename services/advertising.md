## Placements

There are two slots on a page, and you can take either or both:

- __In-content__ - below the article or page body, full width. The highest-attention slot.
- __Sidebar__ - top of the aside column on wide screens, above the page outline.

Placements run sitewide across app pages, guides, and the blog. Slots are limited to keep each advertiser visible.

## Ad specs

- __Image__: PNG, JPG, or SVG. At least 728x90 for in-content, 300x250 for sidebar. Provide 2x versions where possible.
- __Text__: a headline of up to 60 characters and an optional line of up to 100 characters.
- __File size__: under 150KB per image.
- __Link__: one destination URL. Tracking parameters are fine.
- No animation, no autoplay video, no audio, and no third-party tracking scripts.

If you do not have assets ready, send us your logo and a short description and we will design the placement for you at no extra cost.

## Process

1. __You__ [email us](mailto:hey@visnalize.com) with your product, target slot, and preferred duration.
2. __We__ confirm availability and quote the rate.
3. __You__ send your assets, or ask us to design them.
4. __We__ send you a preview on a staging URL for approval.
5. __You__ approve and make the payment.
6. __We__ publish the placement, usually within 1-2 working days.

## What we do not accept

- NSFW, gambling, or grey-niche products.
- Crypto, forex, and "get rich quick" offers.
- Anything misleading, or products we would not use ourselves.
- Ads designed to look like site UI, system dialogs, or download buttons.
- Malware, fake giveaways, and anything requiring a browser extension install.

We reserve the right to decline or remove any ad that does not meet these standards, with a pro-rated refund for unused time.

## FAQs

### Can I see the performance of my ad?

__Yes__. We report impressions and clicks for your placement on request, and automatically at the end of each month. Since the ad is served by us, these numbers are not filtered by an ad network.

### Why is this more expensive than an ad network?

Because you reach more people for it. A network ad on this site is invisible to every visitor running a blocker, and the network keeps a share of what you pay. A native placement reaches all 170K+ page views, and the whole fee goes into the site you are advertising on.

### Can I change my creative mid-flight?

__Yes__, once per month at no charge. Send the new assets and we will swap them within 1-2 working days.

### Do you offer a trial?

__Yes__. Take the weekly rate for one week. If it does not work for you, there is no obligation to continue.

### Can I combine this with a sponsored post?

__Yes__, and it usually works better than either alone. Bundle a [sponsored post](./sponsored-posts.md) with any monthly placement and the post is __half price__. Ask us when you get in touch.

### Is the ad link do-follow?

Ad links are marked `rel="sponsored"`, as required by search engine guidelines. If you want a do-follow link, a [sponsored post](./sponsored-posts.md) is the right service for that.
